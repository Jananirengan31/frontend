crow story

Once upon a time, on a very hot summer day, a crow was flying across the fields. The sun was shining brightly, and the crow became very thirsty. It searched everywhere for water but could not find any. It flew over trees, houses, and gardens, hoping to find something to drink. After a long search, the crow finally saw a small pitcher under a tree. It quickly flew down and looked inside. There was only a little water at the bottom of the pitcher. The crow tried to drink the water, but its beak was too short to reach it. The crow did not give up. It sat quietly and thought for a while. Soon, it noticed some small stones lying nearby. An idea came to its mind. The crow picked up one small stone with its beak and dropped it into the pitcher. Then it picked up another stone, and another. It kept dropping stones into the pitcher. As more stones filled the pitcher, the water slowly rose higher and higher. After some time, the water reached the top. The crow happily drank the cool water until its thirst was gone. Feeling refreshed and happy, the clever crow flew away.

The crow and the pitcher

Once upon a time, there lived a clever crow in a large tree near a peaceful village. Every morning, the crow flew around looking for food. One day, it found a fresh piece of bread near a bakery. The crow was very happy because it wanted to take the bread home and enjoy it. The crow flew to the branch of a tall tree and held the bread tightly in its beak. Just then, a hungry fox was passing by. When the fox saw the bread, it wanted to eat it. The fox knew that it could not climb the tree, so it decided to use a trick. The fox stood under the tree and looked up at the crow. It smiled and said, "Good morning, dear Crow! Your black feathers are so shiny and beautiful. You are the most beautiful bird I have ever seen." The crow listened quietly but did not say anything because the bread was still in its beak. The fox continued, "I have also heard that your voice is sweet and melodious. If you sing just one song for me, I am sure you will be called the Queen of Birds." The crow became very proud. It wanted to show the fox how well it could sing. Forgetting the bread in its beak, the crow opened its mouth and cried, "Caw! Caw!" As soon as it opened its beak, the piece of bread fell to the ground. The clever fox quickly picked up the bread and began to run away. Before leaving, the fox looked back and said, "Thank you for the delicious bread. Remember, never trust people who flatter you for their own benefit." The crow realized that it had been tricked by the fox's false praise. It felt ashamed but also learned an important lesson. From that day onward, the crow never believed anyone who praised it without a reason and always thought carefully before acting. The fox happily ate the bread, and the crow flew away, wiser than before.
