About

Tech companies are generally divided into three business models: Product-based, Software-as-a-Service (SaaS), and Service-based. They differ fundamentally by what they sell, their target audience, and how employees operate on a daily.
Service-Based (IT Consulting/Outsourcing) Companies
These companies do not build their own products; instead, they provide technical expertise, consulting, and development services to other businesses.
Product-Based Companies
These companies build, own, and sell proprietary technology to a mass market or specific industry. The product can be software or hardware.
Software-Based (SaaS) Companies
SaaS is a specific software distribution model where applications are hosted in the cloud and licensed on a subscription basis.
