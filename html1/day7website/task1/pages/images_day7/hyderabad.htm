Top 20 IT & Software Companies in Hyderabad: 2026 List

Srihari Dhondalay
Updated:Feb 6, 2026
12 min read

Hyderabad is one of India’s fastest-growing cities with a strong IT and software ecosystem. The city serves as a key technology hub in Telangana and is one of the most preferred locations for companies. With planned IT corridors like HITEC City, Gachibowli, Financial District, and Nanakramguda, Hyderabad supports large-scale software development. Several IT companies in Hyderabad deliver advanced solutions in AI & ML, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and enterprise software. 

The city continues to scale rapidly, driven by strong government support, modern infrastructure, and global client demand. As of 2025, Hyderabad is home to more than 1274 IT and software companies, ranging from startups to large multinational corporations. Hyderabad also attracts a highly skilled talent pool from leading institutions like IIT Hyderabad, IIIT Hyderabad, and Osmania University. To benefit from the city’s expanding IT ecosystem, many startups choose virtual office registration in Hyderabad. This enables them to establish a professional business presence while avoiding high infrastructure and operational costs.

In this blog, we will explore the top IT and software companies in Hyderabad, along with their key services and areas of expertise.

Top 20 IT and Software Companies in Hyderabad: Detailed List

The following IT and software companies in Hyderabad significantly contribute to Telangana’s economic growth: 

1. Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Tata Consultancy Services offers consulting, IT services, cloud, AI, and digital transformation solutions in Hyderabad. The company has a significant presence in the city’s development and contributes significantly to Telangana’s IT exports.

2. Infosys

Infosys offers IT consulting, digital services, AI, cloud, analytics, and enterprise solutions with major delivery and support operations in Hyderabad. It is one of India’s largest IT services firms and serves global clients across industries.

3. Microsoft

Microsoft offers software, cloud, AI, analytics, and enterprise solutions with a major development and data center presence in Hyderabad. The company supports products like Azure, Microsoft 365, Windows, and AI tools globally, and employs thousands in the city as part of its India team. Hyderabad is home to a large Microsoft campus and new facility expansions.

4. Amazon

Amazon offers cloud computing (AWS), software development, AI solutions, and e‑commerce tech services in Hyderabad. The city hosts one of its largest global campuses, home to tech, development, and support teams, making it a key hub for Amazon’s India operations.

5. Accenture

Accenture provides consulting, technology services, digital transformation, cloud, AI, and innovation solutions with a major innovation hub and delivery presence in Hyderabad. Its Hyderabad centre supports co‑innovation, prototyping, and scaling of advanced tech products and services across industries. 

6. Capgemini

Capgemini provides IT consulting, digital transformation, AI, software development, and business services with large teams in Hyderabad. The company supports enterprise clients globally and is a key part of India’s tech services ecosystem. Capgemini employs thousands in Hyderabad as part of its India operations. 

7. Cognizant

Cognizant provides IT consulting, AI, analytics, and enterprise software solutions with major delivery and technology centers in Hyderabad. The company serves global clients across industries and is a key contributor to India’s IT exports.

8. HCL Technologies

HCL Technologies provides IT consulting, application development, digital transformation, and infrastructure management services in Hyderabad. The company’s solutions for global clients contribute to Telangana’s IT exports and strengthen the city’s technology ecosystem.

9. IBM 

IBM India offers cloud computing, AI, analytics, and cybersecurity solutions in Hyderabad. The multinational brings global IT practices and training programs to the city, generating employment and enhancing the local talent pool.

10. Google 

Google offers software development, cloud computing, AI, machine learning, and enterprise solutions in Hyderabad. Its Hyderabad office focuses on product development, support, and innovation for global services, contributing to India’s IT ecosystem and creating high-skilled employment.

11. Tech Mahindra

Tech Mahindra offers network technology solutions, IT services, and BPO operations in Hyderabad. The company creates numerous local jobs and runs skill development programs, strengthening the city’s IT and technology talent ecosystem.

12. Tanla Platforms Ltd.

Tanla Platforms Ltd. provides cloud communications, messaging, and enterprise software solutions in Hyderabad. The company serves global clients with innovative digital communication services and contributes to India’s IT and telecom technology ecosystem.

13. Cyient Ltd.

Cyient Ltd. provides engineering, IT services, digital solutions, and data analytics in Hyderabad. The company supports global clients in aerospace, transportation, telecom, and manufacturing, contributing to India’s engineering services sector.

14. ValueLabs 

ValueLabs is a global IT services and software development company headquartered in Hyderabad. It provides product development, quality engineering, data & analytics, AI/ML, cloud solutions, and consulting to clients worldwide. The ValueLabs firm has grown from a small team in Hyderabad to a multinational with thousands of employees and global offices. 

15. Oracle

Oracle provides enterprise software, cloud infrastructure, database solutions, AI, and business applications in Hyderabad. The city hosts major development and support teams that serve global clients across industries, strengthening Telangana’s enterprise IT ecosystem.

16. Deloitte

Deloitte provides consulting, technology, risk advisory, audit, tax, and enterprise services with a strong presence in Hyderabad. Its Hyderabad offices house innovation and delivery centres that support global and Indian clients, creating employment and technology leadership opportunities. 

17. Qualcomm India

Qualcomm provides semiconductor designs, wireless technologies, R&D software, and related services with technology presence in Hyderabad. Its Hyderabad centre is one of the company’s largest global offices outside its US headquarters. This centre supports multiple engineering, chip design, IoT, 5G, and embedded software teams. 

18. ADP India

ADP provides payroll processing, HR software, cloud services, and business outsourcing solutions, with a strong presence in Hyderabad. The city hosts development, client support, and technology teams for global clients.

19. Genpact

Genpact provides IT services, business process management (BPM), analytics, and digital transformation solutions in Hyderabad. Its operations support clients worldwide in finance, insurance, healthcare, and manufacturing sectors.

20. Mphasis

Mphasis provides IT services, cloud solutions, AI, application development, and digital transformation with a significant presence in Hyderabad. The company serves global clients across banking, insurance, and enterprise sectors, strengthening Telangana’s IT ecosystem.

These companies highlight Hyderabad’s growing prominence in the IT and software sector. From global giants to rapidly growing local firms, they drive innovation and generate employment in the city. Their presence strengthens the local economy and encourages startups to establish and register their company efficiently in Hyderabad.

Conclusion 

Hyderabad’s IT and software sector continues to grow rapidly, attracting global giants, innovative startups, and skilled professionals. The city’s strong talent pool, modern infrastructure, and supportive ecosystem make it an ideal destination for businesses in technology, software development, and digital services.
