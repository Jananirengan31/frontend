Once upon a time, on a very hot summer day, a crow was flying across the fields. The sun was shining brightly, and the crow became very thirsty. It searched everywhere for water but could not find any . It flew over trees, houses, and gardens, hoping to find something to drink . After a long search, the crow finally saw a small pitcher under a tree. It quickly flew down and looked inside. There was only a little water at the bottom of the pitcher . The crow tried to drink the water, but its beak was too short to reach it . The crow did not give up.
