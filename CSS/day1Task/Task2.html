Freshworks

Freshworks is a cloud-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) company founded in Chennai in 2010 (originally as Freshdesk). It provides AI-powered software for customer relationship management (CRM), IT service management (ITSM), and customer support. The company is listed on the Nasdaq and serves over 74,000 businesses worldwide.Product & Engineering: Roles such as Lead Software Engineer (Full Stack, System, and Machine Learning) and Site Reliability Engineer (SRE).Sales & Marketing: Positions like Senior Business Development Manager, Strategic Customer Success Manager, and Product Marketing Manager.Operations & Leadership: Strategic roles including Security Operations Center (SOC) Manager, Deals Desk Manager, and Sales Operations Manager.Discover current job openings and requirements across these departments using the Freshworks Careers portal.
